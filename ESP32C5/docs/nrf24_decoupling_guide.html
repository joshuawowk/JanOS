This guide shows, in 3D, exactly where the decoupling capacitors go and how every net on the shared radio header runs — the stiff 3V3 delivery an nRF24 needs and the bulk cap a PA/LNA module needs to survive its TX current spikes. The capacitor stack

Three caps, in parallel, across VCC–GND.

All three sit between the module's VCC and GND pins — not somewhere on the rail, at the module. Each covers a different frequency band; together they hold VCC steady from DC to the RF switching edges.

Why each value

100nF
Ceramic X7R. Kills the high-frequency switching noise from the SPI edges and the RF PLL. Lowest inductance — so it goes closest to the pins.
HF bypass
10µF
Ceramic X5R/X7R. Mid-band reservoir for the mode changes (standby→RX→TX). Sits just behind the 100 nF.
mid-band
100µF
Low-ESR bulk (electrolytic/tantalum/ceramic). Absorbs the TX current spikes — the fix for PA/LNA brownout. Required for +PA/LNA, wise for bare.
bulk
What the drop-out was

0x0E → 0xFF was the shared bus, not the caps.

Two tells point away from brownout: the reads happen in power-down (sub-µA — no current spike for a cap to absorb), and the radio recovers on a soft reset that never cycles the always-on 3V3 rail. Primary-source review found the real cause — and it was firmware, not power.

/dev/ttyUSB0 · nrf24probe 2000 ×5
[NRF24-PROBE] 2000kHz  STATUS=0x0E  RF_CH wr0A->rd0A  wr55->rd55  PASS   # POR value, module fresh
[NRF24-PROBE] 2000kHz  STATUS=0xFF  RF_CH wr0A->rdFF  wr55->rdFF  fail   # browned out
[NRF24-PROBE] 2000kHz  STATUS=0xFF  ...  fail
# 0x0E only on the first read after reset  =  it re-powers, works once, sags under load
✓

Root cause (fixed in firmware)

init_sd_card() is reachable from ~25 runtime command paths (save / list / wardrive / portal / file ops, and the Tab5's list_dir). On this SD-less board every failing call ran spi_bus_free(SPI2_HOST) — freeing the bus the radio shares and is actively using — so MISO read 0xFF until the next reset re-ran radio_detect(). Guarded so a live radio's bus is never freed (commit a8ddd33).

⚡

Still decouple — for TX, not for that symptom

Caps could not have fixed the drop-out (those reads draw no current). But a PA/LNA module still browns out on real TX spikes without the 100µF bulk, and every module wants the 100nF + 10µF at its own pins on a stiff 3V3 feed (never a GPIO, never 5 V). Build the stack below for correctness and TX headroom.

Bench rules

Do & don't.

Do

  • ✓Mount all caps at the module's VCC/GND pins, shortest possible leads.
  • ✓Smallest value closest to the pins (100nF), bulk furthest.
  • ✓Feed VCC from the DevKitC 3V3 pin; one solid GND back to C5 GND.
  • ✓Keep the 10k pull-up on CS (GPIO3) and 4.7k pull-down on CE (GPIO4).
  • ✓For PA/LNA: add the 100µF bulk; consider a dedicated 3V3 LDO off 5V.

Don't

  • ×Power VCC from a GPIO or the 5V rail.
  • ×Put the caps out on the rail instead of at the module.
  • ×Rely on long dupont jumpers for VCC/GND under TX current.
  • ×Expect a cap to fix an open or resistive VCC joint — it can't.
  • ×Socket both radios at once — one at a time on the shared header.
